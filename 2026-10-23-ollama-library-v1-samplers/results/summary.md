What each condition really sampled with is in `effective-params.txt`.

- `api-t0`: /api/chat with options.temperature 0
- `api-default`: /api/chat, no options (the model's shipped parameters)
- `v1-default`: /v1/chat/completions, no sampler fields
- `v1-fix`: /v1/chat/completions with the shipped temperature and top_p in the request
