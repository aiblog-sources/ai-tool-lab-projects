Task: First do the OAM DMA from page $02 (the sprite table at $0200-$02FF). Then copy the 256 bytes at $0300-$03FF to consecutive PPU addresses, starting at the address in zero page $00 (low byte) and $01 (high byte). The destination is always inside nametable 0 and the 256 bytes end before $2400.
